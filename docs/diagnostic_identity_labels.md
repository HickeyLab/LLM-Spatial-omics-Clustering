# Current majority-bound diagnostic labels

Figures 4D–E and S6B show reference agreement and fixed-partition majority-bound components. Figure S7 compares the 21-label vocabulary and the original 28-label vocabulary using the same predictions. The models were not rerun with 28 allowed labels.

For a fixed cluster, let A be the fraction of direct reference matches and U its reference-majority fraction. The components are direct reference agreement A, net annotation gap U − A, and within-cluster bound gap 1 − U. They sum to one. Direct agreement includes minority-label matches. These are arithmetic comparisons and do not causally allocate pipeline errors.

Figure 4E and S7 weight clusters by their cell counts. Figure 4D and S6B retain cluster-level displays. The checked captions are preserved in `data/frozen/manuscript_replay/captions.json`. Source-era wording and older S4/S8 numbering remain in the archived audit, not the current figure map.
