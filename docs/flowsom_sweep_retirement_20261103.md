# Historical FlowSOM sweep retirement

Experiment 4, the historical FlowSOM cluster-count sweep, was retired from active publication materials on November 3, 2026 at the author's request. Its different input/preprocessing and SOM configuration do not justify the updated primary FlowSOM partition.

The active Figure S4 contains A–D. Panels A–C retain the same data and typography. The former E metric panel is now D and uses the available full width; its ARI, AMI, Shannon diversity, F1, recall and purity observations are unchanged. The sweep sentence and caption description are removed from the proposed manuscript, and the metric cross-reference changes from S4E to S4D.

The [archive index](../data/historical/flowsom_cluster_count_sweep_20261103/README.md) and [manifest](../data/historical/flowsom_cluster_count_sweep_20261103/manifest.json) retain the exact old numerical table, configuration, plotting sources, A–E artwork and SHA-256 hashes with immutable commit links. Existing frozen data and the October 30 release are unchanged. Historical reconstruction entry points intentionally retain the old panel letters and are marked archival.

Experiments 7, 9 and 14 retain their existing reported results. Their [combined successor protocol](revision/combined_annotation47_protocol.md) is prepared but disabled, awaiting SpatialSort completion and a separate authorized launch.
