## Verified findings

- Primary means-plus-fractions agreement spans **17.42–58.12%**, with fixed-28-label macro-F1 **0.114–0.449**. Astra–Leiden remains the descriptively selected detailed pipeline. Adding fractions increases agreement in eight method/model conditions and reduces it in eight; changes span **−10.25 to +6.50 percentage points**.
- Selected marker names yield lower agreement than all 47 numerical means in **15 of 16** comparisons. Equal-condition mean agreement is **32.52%** for names, **38.10%** for all means and **37.51%** for all means plus fractions. The compact-numerical arm has no final aggregate because its planned DeepSeek–Leiden condition remains incomplete.
- Opus method-name controls reduce agreement in all four methods, by **2.54–5.12 percentage points**. Adding measured neighborhood expression changes Opus–Leiden agreement from **50.27% to 45.97%**. These are matched single-repetition observations, not estimates of a general causal or repeatability effect.
- The conventional classifiers use training-cell reference labels, a richer source of supervision than the LLM prompts. On means-plus-fractions cluster queries, logistic-regression agreement spans **44.09–68.20%**, and nearest-centroid agreement **35.19–55.74%**. Leiden logistic regression reaches **68.20%** versus Astra–Leiden **58.12%** under the primary endpoint.
- Cell-count-weighted Ward coarsening to **K=100 and K=200** preserves the purity ranking Leiden, PIXIE, FlowSOM, SpatialSort under the original 28 labels, while ARI rankings vary. The maps are frozen before reference-label scoring. This is post-clustering sensitivity, not native-K fitting or evidence for stability of LLM annotation rankings.
- Exploratory calibration uses cross-model own-label support with denominator four. Development and evaluation have disjoint cluster IDs and disjoint regions. Mean Brier scores across 16 equally weighted method/model conditions are **0.311 raw, 0.212 recalibrated and 0.229 constant**. Recalibration beats raw support in all 16 conditions but the constant in only 13. Mean calibration error is **0.118 recalibrated versus 0.094 constant**, so improvement is not universal. The calibration population is test-region cells in held-out cluster IDs, distinct from the complete primary test population; the illustrated Astra–Leiden subset contains 42,181 cells. These are retrospective within-donor reference-agreement scores, not native model probabilities or biological confidence.
