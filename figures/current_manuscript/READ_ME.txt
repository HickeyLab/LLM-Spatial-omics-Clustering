Resolution source gap
The original Figure 1A workflow illustrations contain 1765 by 218 source pixels. At their retained 149.4 mm placement width this supplies about 300 dpi. The complete Figure 1 PNG has 600 dpi export metadata, but that does not make the retained raster workflow a native 600 dpi diagram. An editable original, vector export or genuine source crop of at least 3530 by 436 pixels is needed to meet the strict 600 dpi diagram requirement at this size. No higher-resolution detail was manufactured.
